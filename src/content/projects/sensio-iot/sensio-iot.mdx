## Overview

**Sensio IoT** is a smart-space platform that connects software decisions to rooms, devices, telemetry, guest access, and physical control.

The product is organized around explicit organization and room context, making device actions and AI-assisted interactions easier to reason about in shared real-world environments.

## What It Brings Together

- **Room- and organization-scoped device control**
- **Live telemetry surfaces** for connected devices and spaces
- **Guest access flows** for temporary or scoped control
- **AI chat and voice interactions** that can translate user intent into physical actions
- **Purpose-bound streaming and ticket flows** for sensitive real-time interactions
- **Mobile security boundaries** and fail-closed OTA verification
- **Tuya integrations**, broker authentication, plugin supervision, and environment services
- **Observability and deployment infrastructure** for a multi-service IoT system

## Architecture

Sensio IoT is not just a dashboard sitting in front of devices. The platform includes the frontend and backend application layers plus supporting services for broker authorization, plugins, Tuya integration, environment control, and operational observability.

The central design goal is to keep physical actions scoped, attributable, and connected to explicit room or organization context.

## Stack

React 19, Vite, IoT telemetry, AI chat/voice, Tuya, MQTT/broker authentication, Capacitor/mobile surfaces, and Docker-based service infrastructure.
